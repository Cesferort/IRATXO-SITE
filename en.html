How it works

The session

You choose the minutes and the place. Time is measured for real. While it lasts, the apps you marked can wait.

The glade

While you focus, the Iratxoak work. They come back with what the outing gave. The village is something you see, not a table.

On the phone

No accounts, no ads and no server. What you do stays on the device. Nothing is sent to anyone.

A world of its own, kept quiet

The inspiration is Basque mythology, treated with respect. There is no combat and no opponent. You are the protagonist: the Iratxoak help, and the glade exists because you focused.
