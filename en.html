For sitting down to work, and having that show.

IRATXO

Build your world through focus.

You pick the minutes and get on with it. The phone measures that stretch for real. While it lasts, the apps you marked can wait. When you finish, the glade has moved forward: not because you won a round, but because you focused.

How it works

What you do

One outing: how many minutes, and where the Iratxoak go. You stay with whatever you sat down to do. The session is not a game you have to tend.

What they do

While you focus, the Iratxoak work in the glade. They come back with what the outing gave. You can see the village: houses, a walk, short games between sessions. Never during one.

What stays on the phone

No account, no ads and no server. No opponent and no ranking. What you do stays on the device. In Spanish, Basque and English.

Before you install it

It is not in the store yet. This page is here so you can see what it is, and read how it treats data.
