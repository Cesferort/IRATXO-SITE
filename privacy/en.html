Privacy policy

IRATXO runs entirely on the device. There are no accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no ads and no tracking identifiers. Nothing you do in IRATXO is sent to anyone.

What stays on the phone

  • Focus sessions and statistics.
  • The village: resources, buildings, encyclopedia, minigames, ornaments, and the Iratxoak names and colours.
  • Your preferences: language, appearance, sound, vibration, and the apps you do not want to use during a session.
  • A JSON copy only if you export it yourself from Settings.

That storage is local. Uninstalling IRATXO deletes it. There is no automatic cloud copy.

What is read, and only if you allow it

During a focus session, and only then, IRATXO may read which app is in the foreground so it can warn you if it is one you marked. That value lives in memory, is not stored and never leaves the phone. The permission is optional: without it the session still measures time; only the warning is missing.

The overlay and notifications are optional. The ongoing notification exists because Android requires it while a session is running. There are no notifications to pull you back into the app.

IRATXO does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS, or access to every installed app.

Vibration and sound

A short tone and pulse can confirm the end of a glade game. They never play during a focus session. You can turn them off in Settings.

Your rights

You can export or restore your data from Settings, revoke any permission in the system, or delete everything by uninstalling. There is no account to close. The controller is whoever publishes the app on Google Play.
