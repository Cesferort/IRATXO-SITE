Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

IRATXO runs entirely on the device. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no tracking identifiers. Nothing you do in IRATXO is sent to anyone.

What stays on the phone

  • The expeditions: how long they lasted, where they went, what they brought back and what they told on their return.
  • The village: resources, buildings and where each one stands, ornaments bought and placed.
  • The Iratxoak: their names, the clothing colours you bought, the trade each one has been learning, and whether one of them has stayed behind.
  • The encyclopedia and the games: what you have found, what you have read and your best scores.
  • Your preferences: language, appearance, sound, vibration, reduced motion, and the apps you do not want to use during an expedition.
  • A backup of your game, in a file, only if you export it yourself from Settings.

That storage is local. Uninstalling IRATXO deletes it. There is no automatic cloud copy.

What is read, and only if you allow it

During an expedition, and only then, IRATXO may read which app is in the foreground so it can warn you if it is one you marked. That value lives in memory, is not stored and never leaves the phone. The permission is optional: without it the expedition still measures time; only the warning is missing.

The overlay and notifications are optional. The ongoing notification exists because Android requires it while an expedition is running. There are no notifications to pull you back into the app.

IRATXO does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS, or access to every installed app.

Your rights

You can export your game to a file —a backup— and load it again, from Settings, revoke any permission in the system, or delete everything by uninstalling. There is no account to close. The controller is whoever publishes the app on Google Play.
